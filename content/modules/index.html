Writing Your First Playbook

Use the Ansible VS Code extension's AI coding assistant to generate and run playbooks and roles. ~45 min, 6 modules.

Modules

  1. Meet Your Automation Coding Assistant — verify the extension, explore the inventory
  2. Generate a Comprehensive Playbook — craft a prompt, generate system_setup.yml
  3. Understand Your Playbook — walk through variables, tasks, templates, handlers
  4. Run and Verify the Playbook — execute with ansible-navigator, check results
  5. Generate a Role — generate a role, explore the directory structure
  6. Wrap-Up — summary and next steps
